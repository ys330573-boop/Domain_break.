CRITICAL SPARK: press your next attack right after a hit connects (≈0.1s) — 2.5× damage, black lightning and a big boost to your Domain gauge.

DOMAIN EXPANSION: fill the vertical 領域 gauge by fighting, then press Domain. A sure-hit cinematic — then you suffer BURNOUT (no specials for a few seconds).

PHONE / TABLET: left thumb anywhere on the left half = move stick (push up = jump, down = block). Right thumb: hold ATTACK for an automatic combo, HEAVY, DASH, the three specials and DOMAIN (glows when the gauge is full). ❚❚ pauses, ⛶ = fullscreen. Turn the phone sideways.
